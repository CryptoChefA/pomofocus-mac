Cinema mode

Your timer, as a screensaver.

Press ⇧⌘F and the countdown fills the screen: thin rolling digits, the dial unrolled into a minute tape, and soft light drifting behind it. Made for ultrawide monitors, perfect for reading a book with one eye on the clock.

Everything you need

Thoughtful, down to the second.

A complete focus ritual, with none of the noise.

Menu-bar timer

A tiny focus tank that drains as you work, breathes with you, turns green on breaks and blinks red in overtime.

Desktop widgets

Small, medium and large widgets with a live countdown and a single restrained gold progress band.

Day timeline

Every focus block, break and pause laid out on your day, with an optional read-only overlay of your calendar.

Generated ambience

Rain, ocean, brown noise and a soft tick, synthesised on your Mac. No audio files, no streaming.

Breathing ritual

Begin each block with three slow, guided breaths. It's surprisingly hard to start scrolling after that.

Private analytics

Streaks, a 12-week heatmap, focus by hour and weekday, and 7/30/90-day deep dives. All computed locally.

End session, keep it all

Stop a whole sitting with ⌘. and keep every minute you tracked. Abandon always asks first.

Session canvas

Notes and image references for the block at hand: paste, drop, caption and resize, saved with your history.

Light on your Mac

About 8% CPU with a timer running. Motion is handed to Core Animation, and an idle Pomofocus schedules no wakeups.

The end of a sitting

Finish strong. Brava.

Single pomodoros pass quietly. When you end the whole sitting, Pomofocus sends confetti across your screen and lays out what you did.

  • Every pomodoro in order, glowing when it ran the full block
  • Total focus counting up, plus breaks, pauses and overtime
  • Today against your goal, and your streak
  • Sittings that run past midnight still count as one
Know your rhythm

Insight without the guilt.

Analytics that inform rather than shame, and a history you can search, export and own.

Local-first

Your focus is nobody's business.

Pomofocus has no account system, no telemetry, no analytics SDK and no networking code. Everything lives in one human-readable file you can inspect, back up or delete.

~/Library/Application Support/Pomofocus/pomofocus-data.json

The optional calendar overlay only reads events macOS already syncs. Nothing is ever written back.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

Download

Grab the latest build. It's a small zip containing the app and its widget.

Download Pomofocus.zip

Move to Applications

Unzip it and drag Pomofocus into your Applications folder.

Open it the first time

The build isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS will block the first launch. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway, or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine \
  /Applications/Pomofocus.app

Prefer to build it yourself? You'll need macOS 14+ and Swift 6.2 (Xcode 26 or its Command Line Tools).

# clone, build, and open
git clone https://github.com/CryptoChefA/pomofocus-mac.git
cd pomofocus-mac
Scripts/build-app.sh
open outputs/Pomofocus.app
