Privacy

Nothing leaves your Mac.

No account and no telemetry. Your history is one human-readable JSON file you can open, back up or delete. The only thing Pomofocus ever asks the internet is whether a newer version is out, and you can switch that off.

New in 0.22

Plan the day.
Then do it.

Updates itself

When a new version is out, Settings gets a red dot and an Update now button. One click and it reopens on the new version, with everything kept.

A day you can see

Drag tasks into the free time on today's timeline, or let Plan my day fit them around your calendar. Break big tasks into steps, and wrap the day up in one go.

Sounds worth keeping on

Recorded rain, ocean waves and a ticking wall clock, plus soft brown and white noise, all looped so you never hear the seam.

Your kind of celebration

Confetti, fireworks or balloons when you finish a sitting, or a surprise each time.

Rate the session

Say how focused you felt at the end of a sitting, or after every block if you prefer. Each rating sits beside its session in History and feeds your insights.

Split blocks

Switch lists halfway through a block and both halves are counted where they belong, as one block.

The end of a sitting

Brava.

Single pomodoros pass quietly. When you end the whole sitting, confetti, fireworks or balloons fill your screen, and every block you finished is laid out in front of you.

Version 0.22.2 for macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. Free and open source under the MIT license.
Step 1

Download

Get Pomofocus.zip and unzip it.

Step 2

Move to Applications

Drag Pomofocus into your Applications folder.

Step 3

Open it once

This build isn't notarized by Apple, so allow it under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, or run:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Pomofocus.app
Step 4

That's it

From version 0.22.1 on, Pomofocus updates itself: it lets you know when a new version is out, or choose Pomofocus → Check for Updates. To pass it on, use Settings → Share Pomofocus.

Updating Pomofocus

Your sessions, tasks, lists and settings are kept on your Mac and stay exactly as they are when you update.

Version 0.22.1 or newer

One click

When a new version is out, Pomofocus shows Pomofocus is ready. Click Update now: it downloads, closes and reopens on its own.

To check yourself, choose Pomofocus → Check for Updates, or Settings → Updates → Update Pomofocus.

Version 0.22.0 or older

Update once by hand

Quit Pomofocus. Download Pomofocus.zip with the button above, unzip it, and drag Pomofocus into Applications. Choose Replace, then open it.

From then on it updates itself.

Not sure which you have?

Look in Settings

Open Settings: the top line says which version you're on. You can also choose Pomofocus → About Pomofocus.
